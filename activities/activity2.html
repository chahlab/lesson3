Our planet earth

General Quick Facts:

  • Age: Earth is approximately 4.5 billion years old.

  • Water Coverage: About 71% of the surface is covered by liquid water.

  • Shape:It isn't a perfect sphere; it's an oblate spheroid,slightly flattened at the top and bottom.

  • Moons:Earth has exactly one natural satellite, which we just call the Moon.

Layers of the Earth

  1. The Crust:The ultra-thin, rocky outer shell that we actually live on.

  2. The Mantle:A massive, thick layer of hot rock that makes up most of Earth's volume.

  3. The Outer Core: A layer of completely liquid iron and nickel that creates Earth's magnetic field.

  4. The Inner Core:
    5. A scorching hot, solid metal ball at the very center of the planet.

Earth's Core Systems

Atmosphere:
The blanket of gases (mostly nitrogen and oxygen) that surrounds the planet and protects us from space.
Biosphere:
The global ecological network where all living organisms—plants, animals, and humans exist.
Geosphere:
The solid parts of the Earth, including all the rocks, minerals, landforms, and the deep interior layers.
